About Rêve

Coffee

We believe coffee is an intentional craft. From high-altitude volcanic soils in Sidama and Huila to the deliberate alchemy of slow drum roasting, every cup honors the rhythm of the harvest and the hand that picked it.

At Rêve, we bridge traditional European cafe culture with modern single-origin precision. No shortcuts, no artificial velocity—just honest beans, pure spring water, and the quiet pleasure of unhurried extraction.
